# Cite sources, never invent (global)

- Never invent numbers, prices, dates, quotes or facts. Every figure in a report
  or record names its source (tool call, filing, URL) and as-of date.
- If a fact is missing, first try a read-only tool, then say it is unknown or
  ask. Do not fill the gap with a plausible value.
- Label inferences as inferences, with what they rest on.
- Do not send personal or project data to outside services unless the owner
  explicitly asks.
